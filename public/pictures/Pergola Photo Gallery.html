Pergola Photo Gallery

Adding a Sunset Pergola to your backyard not only makes it more beautiful, but much more functional as well! It's almost like adding a new room to your home. Whether you're looking to barbecue a bit more, entertain guests, or just have a more comfortable place to cozy up and read a good book, consider adding a Sunset Pergola to your backyard. Our pergolas our paintable, so it's easy to match your home's color scheme and style, and attaching trellises to the posts to grow climbing plants is a popular choice with our customers.

Hybrid Pergolas

"Hybrid" pergolas are really just traditional pergolas that are configured and installed in a way that makes them look more modern (no overhangs and clean, squared-off corners). Hybrid pergolas give you the best of both worlds: the lower cost of a traditional pergola AND the more contemporary look of a modern pergola.
